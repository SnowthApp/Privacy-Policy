
# Privacy Policy – Snowth

**Effective Date:** May 3, 2025

Snowth respects your privacy.

## 1. No Data Collection
We do **not collect**, store, or transmit any personal information or usage data.  
Snowth is a fully offline game. Your gameplay data (such as scores or settings) is stored **locally on your device only** and is never shared.

## 2. No Analytics or Tracking
The app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.

## 3. No Internet Access Required
Snowth runs entirely offline. The app does not require an internet connection and does not communicate with any servers.

## 4. No Contact Methods
There is no account registration, no support email, and no contact forms available in the app.  
If you have questions or concerns, you can contact us through the App Store page if a developer link is provided.

---

## Changes to This Policy
This policy may be updated in the future if features change. If so, this page will be updated accordingly.
